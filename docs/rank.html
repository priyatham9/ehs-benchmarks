Where do I rank?

Enter one year of recordable cases and hours worked. The page shrinks your TRIR toward the industry rate and places it among OSHA Injury Tracking Application peers, with ranges that show how noisy one year is.

Private by design: the calculation runs in your browser. Your numbers are never sent anywhere. The page downloads only static, public peer data for the sector of your NAICS code.
